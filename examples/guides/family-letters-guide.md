# Family Letters Guide

Treat these as correspondence between people with shared context, not a complete biography. Attend to recipient, date, place, family roles, money, illness, work, silences, jokes, and repeated worries.

Search for names, kinship terms, places, `received`, `enclosed`, `doctor`, `school`, `rent`, `wages`, and seasonal words. Quote selectively and avoid exposing private details that are not needed for the answer.
