# Search Rules

Start with document genres and working vocabulary: `letter`, `memorandum`, `draft`, `petition`, `order`, `case`, `law`, `evidence`, `constitution`, `Union`, `emancipation`, `pardon`, `appointment`, `story`, `joke`, `telegraph`.

Useful path strategies:

| Path | Search vocabulary |
| --- | --- |
| legal-mind | `law OR evidence OR court OR case OR jury OR statute` |
| emancipation-in-motion | `emancipation OR slave OR contraband OR proclamation OR colonization` |
| humor-human-lincoln | `autobiographical OR story OR joke OR anecdote OR plain OR self` |
| war-as-paperwork | `order OR telegram OR appointment OR pardon OR dispatch` |
| incoming-pressure | `petition OR request OR protest OR delegation OR recommendation` |
| public-private-lincoln | `private OR confidential OR draft OR copy OR memorandum` |
| lincoln-after-lincoln | `memorial OR recollection OR posthumous OR anniversary` |

Prefer exact names, offices, dates, recipients, and document types. Fetch before interpreting.
