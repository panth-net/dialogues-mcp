# Selection Rules

Select documents that reveal how Lincoln thought, not merely documents about Lincoln.

High-signal:
- Lincoln-authored drafts, memoranda, notes, letters, legal documents, and orders.
- Documents where Lincoln responds to pressure, requests, criticism, or institutional constraint.
- Legal reasoning, constitutional reasoning, tactical delay, moral conflict, and practical logistics.
- Revision chains, drafts, corrections, jokes, stories, vernacular language, and informal tone.
- Correspondence showing public/private gaps.

Lower-signal:
- Generic printed matter.
- Routine appointments without reasoning.
- Third-party documents that merely mention Lincoln.
- Posthumous commemoration, except for the `lincoln-after-lincoln` path.
