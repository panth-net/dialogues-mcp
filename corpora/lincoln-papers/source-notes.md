# Source Notes

There are two public source layers.

The published-writings layer is "The Papers and Writings of Abraham Lincoln" (Arthur Brooks Lapsley edition, seven volumes covering 1832-1865), downloaded as public-domain plain text from Project Gutenberg. It is an early-1900s edited edition: reliable for what Lincoln published and sent, but its texts are editorial transcriptions, not facsimiles, and volume dates are ranges, not per-document dates.

The manuscript layer is the Library of Congress Abraham Lincoln Papers. Many items have metadata and images; only some have transcriptions or OCR suitable for full-text indexing.

Metadata-only or image-only records are not evidence of passage wording. They may guide curation, but answers must rely on fetched text passages.

Posthumous recollections and commemorative material can be useful for the `lincoln-after-lincoln` path but should not be confused with Lincoln-authored working documents.
