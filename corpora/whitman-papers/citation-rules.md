# Citation Rules

Cite the fetched passage ID plus the document's Whitman Archive ID, title, and date (e.g. "loc.02109, Ralph Waldo Emerson to Walt Whitman, 21 July 1855").

Credit the Walt Whitman Archive (whitmanarchive.org) as the source of the transcriptions when making broad claims, and note that this corpus is a curated sample of the Archive's holdings, not the whole correspondence.

State the document type: a letter (and its recipient), a working notebook, or Emerson's incoming letter. Bracketed dates are editorial and uncertain; keep the brackets when citing them.

Quote from fetched passages only. Where manuscript revision matters (notebook drafts especially), say that deletions and insertions have been flattened to running text in this index and that the Archive's TEI or page images are the authority for manuscript detail.
