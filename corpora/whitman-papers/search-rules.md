# Search Rules

Use names, places, and Civil War-era vocabulary, not modern labels.

Good starting searches:

- `hospital OR ward OR wounded OR sick OR nurse OR bed`
- `mother OR Brooklyn OR George OR Jeff OR money OR rent`
- `Pete OR Doyle OR darling OR boy OR horsecars`
- `Sawyer OR soldier OR regiment OR furlough OR comrade`
- `Leaves of Grass OR book OR edition OR career OR foreground`
- `Camden OR Mickle OR Bucke OR Traubel OR Kennedy`
- `Emerson OR Seward OR Concord OR wit OR wisdom`
- `death OR dying OR grave OR farewell OR sleep`

Useful path strategies:

| Path | Search vocabulary |
| --- | --- |
| civil-war-hospitals | `hospital OR ward OR wounded OR Armory OR soldier OR mother` |
| doyle-and-comradeship | `Pete OR Doyle OR darling OR boy OR ride OR kiss` |
| emerson-and-recognition | `Emerson OR career OR foreground OR wit OR wisdom OR Seward` |
| camden-and-last-years | `Camden OR Bucke OR Traubel OR Kennedy OR sick OR edition` |
| notebooks-and-drafts | `poet OR soul OR body OR effused OR slave OR bed OR pencil` |

Concept translations:

- "war trauma" -> `wounded OR amputation OR death OR suffer OR sad OR sights`
- "same-sex affection" -> `darling OR dear boy OR comrade OR kiss OR love`
- "literary fame" -> `Leaves of Grass OR book OR edition OR career OR papers`
- "caregiving" -> `hospital OR nurse OR sit OR write letters OR gave him`
- "family economy" -> `money OR rent OR wages OR pay OR envelope OR stamps`
- "old age and dying" -> `sick OR grip OR bladder OR bed OR pretty low OR farewell`
- "poetic process" -> `poem OR lines OR soul OR body OR draft OR notebook`

Letters are titled "Sender to Recipient, date" — searching a recipient's surname usually finds both the letters and mentions of the person. Whitman abbreviates and dashes freely ("Dr B", "y'rs", "&"); prefer short distinctive words over exact phrases, and widen with OR sets when a first search is thin.
