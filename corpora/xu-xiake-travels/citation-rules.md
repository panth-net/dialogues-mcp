# Citation Rules

Cite the fetched passage ID plus the diary name and the date as it appears in the passage (reign-year dates, e.g. 崇禎十年 for 1637), and name the text layer: the Chinese Wikisource transcription for downloaded chapters, or the edition the user imported.

Quote the Chinese text as fetched; if you add an English translation, label it as your own translation, since no translation is part of this corpus.

Distinguish Xu Xiake's diary text from editorial apparatus. In downloaded Wikisource chapters, bracketed numbers like [1] and the numbered notes at chapter end are modern Wikisource footnotes, and the navigation and license lines are site apparatus — never quote any of these as diary text. Other editions travel with prefaces, collation notes, and annotations of their own. If the passage sits in a known reconstructed or disputed part of the transmission, say so rather than citing it as if it were beyond doubt.
