# Search Rules

The downloaded text is the Chinese Wikisource transcription in traditional characters (遊 not 游, 黃 not 黄, 巖 alongside 岩). Always try the traditional form first; simplified-character searches will usually return nothing.

Write terms naturally: `黃山` and the spaced form `"黃 山"` both work (the engine expands unspaced Chinese runs automatically). Keep terms short — two or three characters — and combine angles with OR.

Good starting searches:

- `洞 OR 巖 OR 岩` (caves and grottoes)
- `"金 沙 江" OR "岷 江" OR "江 源"` (the Yangtze-source argument)
- `"桂 林" OR "陽 朔" OR 灕` (Guilin karst country)
- `"麗 江" OR "大 理" OR "永 昌"` (Yunnan destinations)
- `"靜 聞" OR "顧 僕" OR 僧` (companions on the road)
- `"黃 山" OR "天 台" OR "雁 宕"` (the early mountain diaries)
- `"盤 江" OR "瀾 滄"` (southwest river systems)
- `宿 OR 飯 OR 里` (the daily travel routine)

Concept translations:

- "karst cave survey" -> `洞 OR 巖 OR 乳 OR 炬`
- "river source debate" -> `"江 源" OR "金 沙" OR 岷 OR 溯`
- "mountain climbing" -> `登 OR 峰 OR 頂 OR 崖`
- "daily logistics" -> `里 OR 宿 OR 飯 OR 雨 OR 憩`
- "being robbed on the road" -> `盜 OR 劫 OR 舟`
- "Buddhist sites" -> `寺 OR 庵 OR 僧 OR 塔`

Variant forms matter even within traditional text (巖/岩, 樑/梁, 宕/蕩). If a search returns nothing, swap the variant character before concluding the topic is absent — and remember this download is sixteen chapters, not the whole Youji, so a missing topic may simply sit in a chapter that is not downloaded.
