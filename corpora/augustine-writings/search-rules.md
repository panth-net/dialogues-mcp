# Search Rules

Use the vocabulary of the 19th-century translations and Augustine's own recurring words, plus proper names. The Pusey *Confessions* says "Thou," "continency," "vouchsafe"; the Dods *City of God* says "the earthly city," "the heavenly city."

Good starting searches:

- `pears OR theft OR stole OR sin OR pleasure`
- `garden OR "take up and read" OR Ponticianus OR Antony OR fig`
- `memory OR palaces OR fields OR remember OR forgetfulness`
- `time OR present OR past OR future OR eternity`
- `Monica OR mother OR Ostia OR tears OR died`
- `Rome OR Alaric OR sack OR barbarians OR pagan OR gods`
- `"earthly city" OR "heavenly city" OR "city of God" OR Babylon OR Jerusalem`
- `Jerome OR Donatist OR letter OR bishop OR brethren`
- `memoria OR tempus OR voluntas OR "tolle lege"` (Latin text)

Useful path strategies:

| Path | Search vocabulary |
| --- | --- |
| conversion-and-the-will | `will OR converted OR garden OR chastity OR continency OR Ponticianus` |
| memory-and-time | `memory OR time OR present OR eternity OR forgetfulness OR palaces` |
| grief-and-friendship | `friend OR death OR tears OR Monica OR Ostia OR Adeodatus` |
| sack-of-rome-and-two-cities | `Rome OR Alaric OR sack OR pagan OR "earthly city" OR "heavenly city"` |
| pastoral-letters | `Jerome OR Donatist OR letter OR Nebridius OR bishop OR church` |

Concept translations:

- "procrastination / weakness of will" -> `not yet OR delay OR chastity OR will OR bound`
- "introspection" -> `memory OR inward OR heart OR myself OR abyss`
- "philosophy of time" -> `time OR present OR measure OR eternity OR passing`
- "bereavement" -> `death OR tears OR grief OR friend OR mother`
- "theology of history" -> `"two cities" OR earthly OR heavenly OR Babylon OR pilgrimage`
- "church conflict" -> `Donatist OR schism OR heretic OR unity OR rebaptize`
- "checking the translation" -> search the English first, then the Latin *Confessiones* with `memoria OR tempus OR voluntas OR peccatum`

Book positions help: pears are Book II, the friend's death Book IV, the garden Book VIII, Ostia Book IX, memory Book X, time Book XI. If a search misses, try the Victorian spelling or diction ("Thou madest us", "restless"). The letters volume is rough OCR — prefer distinctive proper names there over common words.
