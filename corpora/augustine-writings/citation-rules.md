# Citation Rules

Cite the fetched passage ID, the work, and the book number when it is visible in the passage (e.g. *Confessions* Book VIII; *The City of God* Book XIX).

Name the translation when making broad claims: Pusey (1838) for the *Confessions*, Dods (1871) for *The City of God*, Sparrow-Simpson's 1919 selection for the letters. If an argument hangs on a single English word, check and cite the parallel passage in the Latin *Confessiones* before treating the word as Augustine's.

In the letters volume, separate Augustine's translated words from Sparrow-Simpson's surrounding commentary, and flag OCR damage rather than silently correcting a quotation.

Distinguish what Augustine narrates from what he interprets — the *Confessions* retells events decades later through a theological lens, and citations should not present the interpretation as a contemporaneous record.
