# Search Rules

These searches serve users who have imported a permitted transcription. The index is empty until then. Search in the script of the text you imported: kanbun original, modern Japanese, or English translation.

Good starting searches:

- `赤気 OR aurora OR "red vapor"`
- `客星 OR "guest star" OR 彗星 OR comet`
- `除目 OR 昇進 OR promotion OR appointment`
- `歌合 OR "poetry contest" OR 和歌`
- `新古今 OR "Shin Kokinshu" OR 撰集`
- `咳 OR 病 OR ill OR cough OR fever`
- `院 OR 後鳥羽 OR Go-Toba`
- `月 OR moon OR 天変 OR omen`

Useful path strategies:

| Path | Search vocabulary |
| --- | --- |
| sky-and-omens | `赤気 OR 客星 OR 彗星 OR 月食 OR aurora OR comet OR eclipse` |
| court-and-career | `除目 OR 昇進 OR 院 OR 内裏 OR promotion OR audience OR rank` |
| poetry-and-canon | `歌合 OR 和歌 OR 撰集 OR 定家 OR contest OR anthology` |
| illness-and-daily-life | `病 OR 咳 OR 火事 OR 雨 OR ill OR fire OR rain` |

Concept translations:

- "career frustration" -> `除目 OR 昇進 OR 恨 OR passed over OR rank`
- "astronomical observation" -> `赤気 OR 客星 OR 彗星 OR 月 OR star OR sky`
- "literary canon-making" -> `撰集 OR 和歌 OR 百人一首 OR anthology OR selection`
- "chronic illness" -> `病 OR 咳 OR 不快 OR cough OR fever OR pain`
- "politics after the coup" -> `承久 OR 院 OR 幕府 OR exile OR shogunate`
- "daily Kyoto life" -> `火事 OR 雨 OR 地震 OR fire OR famine OR weather`

Dates run by era name (Jisho, Kennin, Jokyu, Kanki). If a Western-calendar search fails, try the era name and month.
