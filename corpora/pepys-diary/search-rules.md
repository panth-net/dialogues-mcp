# Search Rules

Use Restoration-period vocabulary and proper names, not modern labels.

Good starting searches:

- `plague OR sickness OR dead OR buried OR infection`
- `fire OR burned OR Tower OR flames OR goods`
- `Navy OR office OR Deptford OR victualling OR contract`
- `Dutch OR fleet OR Medway OR Chatham OR war`
- `wife OR Deb OR quarrel OR jealous OR peace`
- `play OR playhouse OR musique OR song OR viall`
- `money OR worth OR accounts OR gold OR purse`
- `King OR Duke OR Whitehall OR Council OR Parliament`

Useful path strategies:

| Path | Search vocabulary |
| --- | --- |
| plague-and-fire | `plague OR infection OR fire OR burned OR flames` |
| navy-office-and-work | `Navy OR office OR victualling OR contract OR Medway` |
| marriage-and-household | `wife OR servant OR quarrel OR jealous OR home` |
| theatre-music-and-pleasure | `play OR playhouse OR musique OR wine OR dinner` |
| money-and-ambition | `worth OR accounts OR money OR gold OR place` |
| restoration-politics | `King OR Duke OR Whitehall OR Parliament OR Council` |

Concept translations:

- "work-life balance" -> `office OR play OR vows OR pleasure OR business`
- "career ambition" -> `place OR preferment OR worth OR interest OR favour`
- "epidemic life" -> `plague OR sickness OR shut OR buried OR Bill of Mortality`
- "disaster response" -> `fire OR goods OR carts OR King OR blow up`
- "marital conflict" -> `wife OR angry OR jealous OR Deb OR peace`
- "self-improvement" -> `vows OR oath OR wine OR playhouse OR fine`

Pepys spells phonetically and inconsistently ("musique", "viall", "tooke"). If a modern spelling returns little, try period spellings or a broader OR set.
