# Citation Rules

Cite the fetched passage ID and the letter number, place, and date visible in the passage (e.g. "No. 9, Hoornkrans, 10 Maart 1889").

Name the edition when making broad claims: the 1929 Van Riebeeck Society edition of Witbooi's captured letter-book, as digitized by DBNL, and note that Witbooi's own texts end in 1894.

Separate the three voices: Witbooi's Cape Dutch letters, the Afrikaans editorial footnotes, and the English introduction and Voigts foreword. Never cite the editorial apparatus as Witbooi's words; its colonial bias must be flagged when it is quoted at all.

When quoting Witbooi's Dutch, give an English gloss and mark it as the answer's own translation, since translation adds uncertainty.
