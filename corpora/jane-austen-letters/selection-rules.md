# Selection Rules

Select records that show Austen's practical thinking in correspondence: family logistics, health, money, publication, wit, social judgment, and ordinary constraint.

High-signal:
- Letters with recipient/date context.
- Passages where practical detail and judgment appear together.
- Publication and authorship references.
- Evidence of family obligation, illness, or money.

Lower-signal:
- Later commentary without direct letter text.
- Novel excerpts presented as biography.
- Isolated famous lines without surrounding letter context.
