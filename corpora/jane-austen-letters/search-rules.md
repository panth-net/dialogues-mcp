# Search Rules

Use family, practical, and social vocabulary.

Good starting searches:

- `Cassandra OR Fanny OR Henry OR Frank OR Edward OR mother`
- `money OR pounds OR paid OR profit OR publication OR publisher`
- `gown OR ball OR visit OR chaise OR Bath OR Chawton`
- `illness OR sick OR health OR death OR pain OR fever`
- `joke OR laugh OR ridiculous OR impertinent OR civil`
- `novel OR proof OR fame OR author OR publication`

Useful path strategies:

| Path | Search vocabulary |
| --- | --- |
| cassandra-and-family | `Cassandra OR mother OR Fanny OR letter OR dear` |
| wit-and-social-judgment | `joke OR laugh OR ridiculous OR impertinent OR civil OR manners` |
| writing-publication-and-money | `publication OR publisher OR proof OR novel OR profit` |
| illness-loss-and-limitation | `illness OR sick OR health OR death OR pain OR fever` |
| selection-and-silence | `collection OR series OR letters OR selected OR Brabourne` |

Concept translations:

- "inner life" -> `think OR hope OR afraid OR sorry OR glad OR vexed`
- "writing career" -> `novel OR proof OR publisher OR publication OR profit`
- "social judgment" -> `civil OR impertinent OR ridiculous OR pleasant OR manners`
- "family pressure" -> `mother OR sister OR brother OR Cassandra OR Henry OR Fanny`
- "what is missing" -> `selected OR omitted OR destroyed OR gap OR letters`
- "snarky" or "lol" -> `joke OR laugh OR ridiculous OR impertinent OR civil OR manners`
- "paid" or "hobby" -> `novel OR proof OR publisher OR publication OR profit OR pounds`

When the user asks about a famous novel, search the letters for writing/publication terms first, then explain whether the fetched evidence supports the connection.
