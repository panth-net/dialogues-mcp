# Search Rules

Use source vocabulary first, then widen.

Good starting searches:

- `walk OR walking OR saunter OR woods OR field OR pond`
- `journal OR morning OR spring OR winter OR ice OR beans OR labor`
- `letter OR Emerson OR Blake OR friendship OR brother OR sister`
- `economy OR house OR work OR labor OR money OR necessity`
- `conscience OR state OR government OR slave OR war OR prison`
- `Maine OR river OR canoe OR Indian OR guide OR mountain`

- "mindfulness" -> `attention OR observe OR morning OR stillness OR walk`
- "minimalism" -> `economy OR necessary OR labor OR house OR beans`
- "political resistance" -> `conscience OR state OR government OR prison OR slave`
- "environmental thought" -> `woods OR pond OR field OR wild OR walking OR nature`

- "tiny house" or "minimalist" -> `economy OR necessary OR labor OR house OR beans OR work`

Prefer path-specific searches when the user names a problem. Use `journals-and-daily-observation` for raw daily seeing, `letters-and-relationships` for social evidence, and `conscience-and-public-duty` for public moral reasoning.
