# Selection Rules

Select documents that show Thoreau thinking in motion: dated observation, recurring field terms, correspondence, moral pressure, public argument, and the transformation of daily notes into essays.

High-signal:
- Journals with dated observation.
- Letters that show relationships, ordinary work, money, illness, and intellectual exchange.
- Essays that convert lived observation or moral conflict into public argument.
- Travel and field prose that keeps attention, method, and encounter visible.

Lower-signal:
- Later anthologies that strip date, genre, or source context.
- Biographical commentary without direct Thoreau text.
- Famous excerpts without surrounding passage context.
