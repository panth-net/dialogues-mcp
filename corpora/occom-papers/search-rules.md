# Search Rules

Search for eighteenth-century mission-world vocabulary and proper names, not modern labels. Occom spells phonetically and capitalizes freely ("Sabbath", "Heathen", "Indien", "Larning").

Good starting searches:

- `Mohegan OR Montauk OR Oneida OR Brothertown`
- `Wheelock OR school OR scholars OR charity`
- `Heathen OR Christian OR baptized OR converted`
- `preached OR sermon OR Sabbath OR meeting`
- `money OR wages OR support OR poverty OR service`
- `England OR London OR collections OR mission`
- `drunkenness OR liquor OR sin OR death OR Moses Paul`
- `land OR lease OR petition OR Assembly OR tribe`

Useful path strategies:

| Path | Search vocabulary |

Concept translations:

- "racial discrimination" -> `poor Indian OR despised OR unequal OR wages OR white`
- "religious conversion" -> `awakened OR converted OR baptized OR new birth OR Heathen`
- "fundraising" -> `collections OR charity OR donations OR England OR subscriptions`
- "institutional betrayal" -> `college OR alba mater OR grand OR money OR school`
- "alcohol and colonialism" -> `drunkenness OR liquor OR rum OR intemperance OR sin`
- "indigenous sovereignty" -> `tribe OR land OR council OR petition OR our people`
- "daily ministry" -> `preached OR Sabbath OR exhorted OR meeting OR baptized`

Dates matter: the break with Wheelock concentrates in letters of 1771-1772; Brothertown material concentrates in journals and petitions of 1784-1787. If a modern spelling returns little, try period spellings or a broader OR set.
