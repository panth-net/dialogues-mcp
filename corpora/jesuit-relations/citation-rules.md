# Citation Rules

Search results and fetched passages use stable IDs:

`corpus:jesuit-relations:doc:<doc_id>:chunk:<page_num>`

When answering, cite the document ID and passage/page number returned by the tool. If a title is available, include it.

When the fetched passage or metadata identifies a source language, translation, edition, or editorial note, mention it briefly only when it affects interpretation or citation clarity. Do not let language caveats replace the substantive answer.

Example:

> "quoted passage" (The Jesuit Relations, `jesuits05jesuuoft`, passage 142)

Do not cite a passage unless it was fetched. Snippets are for choosing what to fetch, not for making deep claims.
