# Search Rules

## Search both French and English

The corpus is bilingual. Search both languages with short OR queries.

Good:

- `liberty OR liberte OR freedom OR libre`
- `council OR conseil`
- `chief OR captain OR capitaine OR chef`

Bad:

- `liberty`
- `indigenous democratic governance voting rights`

## Use short phrases

FTS queries work best with one concept at a time. Use short phrases of one to three words and separate alternatives with OR. Long modern word lists usually fail because the exact combination will not appear in one passage.

## Translate modern concepts into historical vocabulary

Modern user wording often does not match 17th- and 18th-century source vocabulary.

- Democracy: search `council OR conseil OR assembly OR vote OR voices`
- Human rights: search `liberty OR liberte OR custom OR coutume OR law OR loi`
- Government: search `authority OR power OR captain OR capitaine OR chief`
- Police: search `punishment OR justice OR law OR loi`
- Economy: search `trade OR commerce OR beaver OR castor OR gift`
- Inequality: search `poor OR pauvre OR rich OR riche OR master OR servant`

## Think historically

Ask what a Jesuit missionary, colonial administrator, Wendat speaker, or translator would actually have said. Search for the conversation that likely happened, not just the user's modern abstraction.

## Search for gaps

Null results matter. If several reasonable searches fail, report the gap instead of forcing a claim. Try alternate spellings and OCR variants before concluding.
