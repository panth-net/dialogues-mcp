# Source Notes

The Jesuit Relations are missionary and colonial documents. They are valuable primary sources, but not neutral descriptions.

Important limitations:

- Missionaries wrote for European religious and political audiences.
- Indigenous speech is usually translated, summarized, or framed by Jesuit authors.
- The Thwaites edition adds editorial structure and translation choices.
- OCR can introduce broken words, missing accents, bad punctuation, and page artifacts.
- Old terms may have meanings different from present usage.

Use the corpus to quote and analyze what the documents record. Avoid treating the record as a complete account of Indigenous societies.

Local install/download notes:

- The preferred local index is built from the existing Jesuit seed SQL when available.
- `dialogues corpus download jesuit-relations --yes` downloads Internet Archive OCR text into ignored local data storage.
- Search metadata records Internet Archive item URLs when known.
