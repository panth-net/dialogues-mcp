# Selection Rules

Select documents that show scientific thought before closure.

High-signal:
- Questions, doubts, experiments, observations, and correspondent exchange.
- Evidence of theory changing through networked correspondence.
- Domestic, illness, or logistical constraints affecting work.
- Marginalia and reading records that show influence or active engagement.

Lower-signal:
- Finished doctrine without working evidence.
- Generic biography or commemorative material.
- Metadata-only records unless they document a key relationship or missing source boundary.
