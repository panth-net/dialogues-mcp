# Search Rules

Search with practical scientific vocabulary: `species`, `variation`, `experiment`, `observation`, `specimen`, `breeding`, `pigeon`, `barnacle`, `orchid`, `illness`, `letter`, `doubt`, `query`.

| Path | Search vocabulary |
| --- | --- |
| thought-before-theory | `query OR doubt OR difficulty OR problem OR hypothesis` |
| species-and-variation | `species OR variation OR varieties OR selection OR breeding` |
| experiments-and-observations | `experiment OR observation OR specimen OR measured OR trial` |
| network-of-correspondents | `letter OR correspondent OR Hooker OR Lyell OR Gray` |
| illness-work-and-family | `Caroline OR Father OR home OR Cambridge OR specimens OR work` |
| marginalia-and-reading | `marginalia OR notes OR read OR book OR marked` |
| public-private-science | `publish OR private OR confidential OR draft OR review` |

Search by correspondent, date, and path when metadata supports it.

Current installed evidence for `illness-work-and-family` is stronger for family
and working context than for direct illness evidence. For a health-specific
question, also search the whole corpus for `health OR sick OR illness OR unwell`
and state whether those hits fall outside the path.
