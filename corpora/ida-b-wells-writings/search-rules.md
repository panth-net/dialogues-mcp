# Search Rules

Use the period's own vocabulary and proper names, not modern labels. The pamphlets say "lynch law," "mob," "outrage," "alleged," "colored," and "Afro-American"; they rarely use modern analytical terms.

Good starting searches:

- `lynched OR lynching OR "lynch law" OR mob OR hanged OR burned`
- `Moss OR McDowell OR Stewart OR "People's Grocery" OR Memphis`
- `alleged OR charge OR rape OR assault OR "usual charge" OR excuse`
- `statistics OR record OR tabulated OR Tribune OR cases`
- `"Free Speech" OR press OR editor OR newspaper OR published`
- `boycott OR "street car" OR Oklahoma OR business OR property`
- `Winchester OR defense OR protection OR resistance`
- `"Robert Charles" OR "New Orleans" OR police OR riot`

Useful path strategies:

| Path | Search vocabulary |
| --- | --- |
| case-lists-and-statistics | `lynched OR statistics OR record OR Tribune OR cases OR alleged` |
| unmasking-the-usual-charge | `rape OR charge OR alleged OR excuse OR "thread bare" OR white women` |
| economics-boycott-and-press | `business OR grocery OR boycott OR "street car" OR press OR Oklahoma` |
| self-defense-and-remedies | `Winchester OR defense OR remedy OR investigation OR agitation OR duty` |

Concept translations:

- "racial terror" -> `lynched OR mob OR burned OR hanged OR shot OR outrage`
- "false accusation" -> `alleged OR charge OR excuse OR "usual charge" OR pretext`
- "economic motive" -> `grocery OR business OR competition OR property OR prosperous`
- "press freedom" -> `"Free Speech" OR editor OR press OR destroyed OR exile`
- "collective action" -> `boycott OR emigration OR Oklahoma OR "street car" OR meeting`
- "armed self-defense" -> `Winchester OR rifle OR defense OR protection OR resistance`
- "due process denied" -> `trial OR court OR jury OR law OR justice OR untried`

Victim names are the most precise entry points into the case lists: search a name from *The Red Record*'s tables (e.g. `Henry Smith OR Paris Texas`, `Lee Walker`, `C.J. Miller`) to reach the full documented account. If a modern term returns little, translate it into what an 1890s newspaper would have printed.
