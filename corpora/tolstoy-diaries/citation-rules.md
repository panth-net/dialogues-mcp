# Citation Rules

Cite the fetched passage ID, the Jubilee edition volume (e.g. "PSS vol. 52"), and the entry date visible in the passage (e.g. "22 мая 1891 г."). Note that entry dates are Old Style.

Quote the Russian first, then give a translation and mark it as the assistant's translation. Keep the square-bracket editorial expansions ("к[оторый]") as printed when quoting exactly.

Separate Tolstoy's diary text from the Soviet editors' prefaces, commentary, and variant apparatus, which share the same volume text stream. When a passage may be editorial rather than diary, say so.

The text layer is OCR; if a quoted reading looks garbled, flag the uncertainty instead of silently correcting it.
