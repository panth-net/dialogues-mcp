# Source Notes

The curated core is diary volumes of the 90-volume Jubilee edition of Tolstoy's complete works (Polnoe sobranie sochinenii, Moscow, 1928-1958), in the per-volume PDF scans hosted on tolstoy.ru. The digitization is the "Vsyo Tolstoy v odin klik" ("All of Tolstoy in one click") project of the State L.N. Tolstoy Museum and ABBYY, in which several thousand volunteers proofread ABBYY FineReader OCR of every volume; the project released the files for free public use.

Rights nuance, stated plainly:

- Tolstoy died in 1910; his own diary text is public domain everywhere.
- The Jubilee edition volumes also contain Soviet-era editorial matter — prefaces, commentary, variant apparatus, indexes — whose compilers died much later, and that apparatus may still carry rights in some jurisdictions. The manifest therefore marks these volumes `no_known_restrictions` rather than `public_domain`: the digitization was released for free use and the core text is unquestionably free, but treat the editorial apparatus as quotable-with-caution and keep the downloaded files for local personal indexing.

Known limits of this source:

- The searchable text is an OCR text layer over page images. Expect hyphenation joins, occasional character confusion, and running heads ("ДНЕВНИК", dates, page numbers) inside the text stream.
- Editorial expansions of Tolstoy's abbreviations are printed in square brackets ("ч[то]", "к[оторый]"), which splits words for exact search.
- Dates are Old Style (Julian). Tolstoy's 28 October 1910 departure is 10 November in the Gregorian calendar.
- The diaries occupy roughly volumes 46-58 of the edition; this capsule selects three volumes (46, 52, 58) and lists further volumes as curator candidates in full-dev mode. Absence of a period from search results usually means the volume is not downloaded, not that Tolstoy was silent.

Downloaded PDFs are stored in the user's Dialogues data folder and indexed into a local search file. No source text or generated index is bundled.
