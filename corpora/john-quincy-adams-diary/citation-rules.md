# Citation Rules

Cite the fetched passage ID and the entry date visible in the passage (e.g. "24 February 1841").

State which text the passage comes from: the downloaded text here is the Charles Francis Adams Memoirs edition (1874-1877), so cite the Memoirs volume (e.g. "Memoirs, vol. X") alongside the date. If a user has separately imported text from the MHS/Primary Source Cooperative Digital Diary transcription or another source, name that edition instead. Editions differ in coverage and cuts.

The Memoirs text is uncorrected OCR: quote what the passage actually shows, and flag garbled names, dates, or numbers rather than silently correcting them. For wording that matters, recommend checking the Digital Diary online.

Never cite catalog records as if they were diary text. If a claim rests only on a `catalog_only` record (the Digital Diary or Adams Papers portal), say that the record identifies the material but provides no passage evidence.

Separate Adams's words from editorial apparatus — Charles Francis Adams's introductions, notes, and the volume I memoir are the editor's voice — and flag uncertainty when the entry may exist in both a short line-a-day version and an expanded version.
