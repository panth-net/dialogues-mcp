# Search Rules

The indexed text is the twelve-volume Memoirs edition in uncorrected OCR. Use period vocabulary and proper names, not modern labels — Adams writes formally and names people precisely — and if a term returns nothing, try variant spellings before concluding it is absent: OCR mangles names and numbers.

Good starting searches:

- `petition OR gag OR abolition OR slavery OR censure`
- `Amistad OR Africans OR "Supreme Court" OR argument`
- `Ghent OR treaty OR negotiation OR minister OR despatch`
- `Monroe OR cabinet OR Spain OR Florida OR Onis`
- `Clay OR Jackson OR Calhoun OR Webster OR Congress`
- `swim OR Potomac OR bathe OR sunrise OR walk`
- `observatory OR astronomy OR comet OR Smithsonian OR eclipse`
- `disappointment OR indolence OR wasted OR resolution OR prayer`

Useful path strategies:

| Path | Search vocabulary |
| --- | --- |
| diplomacy-and-cabinet | `Ghent OR treaty OR minister OR despatch OR Monroe OR cabinet` |
| presidency-and-party-battles | `election OR bargain OR Clay OR Jackson OR administration OR message` |
| antislavery-house-years | `petition OR gag OR Amistad OR censure OR Texas OR annexation` |
| self-scrutiny-and-science | `swim OR indolence OR resolution OR observatory OR Bible OR astronomy` |

Concept translations:

- "antislavery politics" -> `petition OR gag OR abolition OR Texas OR annexation`
- "political enemies" -> `Jackson OR Calhoun OR faction OR censure OR conspiracy`
- "self-improvement" -> `resolution OR indolence OR early rising OR temperance OR Bible`
- "science policy" -> `observatory OR weights OR measures OR Smithsonian OR longitude`
- "career disappointment" -> `disappointment OR mortification OR defeat OR neglect`
- "daily routine" -> `swim OR walk OR rose OR breakfast OR evening`

If a modern term returns little, try the institutional noun Adams would use: `the House`, `the Department`, `the mission`, `the Court`.
