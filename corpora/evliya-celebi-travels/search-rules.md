# Search Rules

Use Hammer's period spellings and Ottoman titles, not modern place names or labels.

Good starting searches:

- `Constantinople OR walls OR gates OR talisman OR column`
- `mosque OR minaret OR cupola OR marble OR college`
- `Sultan OR vezir OR mufti OR janissaries OR palace`
- `siege OR conquest OR cannon OR fleet OR fortress`
- `Tatar OR Crimea OR Azak OR Cossacks OR khan`
- `Trebisonde OR Erzerum OR Tabriz OR Sinope OR stations`
- `dervish OR sheikh OR tomb OR pilgrimage OR miracle`
- `storm OR ship OR harbour OR anchor OR waves`

Useful path strategies:

Concept translations:

- "urban description" -> `mosque OR bath OR market OR khan OR quarter`
- "religious life" -> `dervish OR sheikh OR pilgrimage OR tomb OR prayer`
- "military campaign" -> `siege OR cannon OR fortress OR troops OR standard`
- "ethnography" -> `language OR inhabitants OR dress OR customs OR complexion`
- "trade and food" -> `merchants OR market OR fruits OR provisions OR wine`
- "danger on the road" -> `storm OR robbers OR waves OR frozen OR escaped`

Hammer's transliterations are idiosyncratic: Trebisonde (Trabzon), Erzerum (Erzurum), Azak or Assov (Azov), Brussa (Bursa), Kostantineh (Istanbul). Diacritics in the text (Sultán, Evliyá, Erzerúm) still match plain-letter searches. If a modern place name fails, try the nineteenth-century form or a broader OR set.
