# Citation Rules

Cite the fetched passage ID plus the volume (I: Istanbul; II: the journeys) and the place or section visible in the passage.

Attribute the English wording to the von Hammer translation (1834/1850) when making broad claims; it is dated and sometimes loose, so avoid resting fine points on a single translated word.

Report marvels, talismans, and huge numbers as Evliya's claims in Evliya's voice — genre features, not verified fact — without silently correcting them.

When a topic falls outside these two volumes, say that eight of the ten volumes of the Seyahatname are not included here rather than concluding Evliya never wrote about it.
