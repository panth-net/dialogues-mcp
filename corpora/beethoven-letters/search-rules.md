# Search Rules

Use music-work vocabulary together with practical pressure vocabulary.

Good starting searches:

- `publisher OR proof OR score OR copyist OR subscription OR dedication`
- `sonata OR quartet OR symphony OR opera OR Mass OR concerto`
- `Archduke OR Ries OR Wegeler OR brother OR nephew OR Karl`
- `illness OR health OR deaf OR hearing OR doctor`
- `money OR florins OR ducats OR payment OR debt`
- `patron OR Prince OR Count OR appointment OR benefit`

Useful path strategies:

| Path | Search vocabulary |
| --- | --- |
| composition-and-publishers | `publisher OR proof OR score OR copyist OR subscription OR dedication` |

Concept translations:

- "creative process" -> `score OR proof OR copyist OR arrangement OR quartet OR sonata`
- "business of art" -> `publisher OR subscription OR payment OR dedication OR proof`
- "illness and isolation" -> `health OR illness OR deaf OR hearing OR doctor`
- "family pressure" -> `brother OR nephew OR Karl OR guardian OR mother`
- "patronage" -> `Archduke OR Prince OR Count OR patron OR dedication`
- "deaf sad genius" -> `illness OR health OR deaf OR hearing OR doctor OR suffer`

For original German imports, pair English search terms with German equivalents discovered in fetched context. The answer can translate, but citation must preserve the source language.
