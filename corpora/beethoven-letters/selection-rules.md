# Selection Rules

Select records that show Beethoven dealing with concrete pressures around music, money, health, patrons, publishing, family, and reputation.

High-signal:
- Letters to publishers, copyists, patrons, friends, and family.
- Passages about composition, proofing, arrangement, performance, and dedication.
- Health, deafness, money, guardianship, or legal pressure with recipient context.
- Original-language imports when rights and provenance are clear.

Lower-signal:
- Biographical summary without direct letter evidence.
- Translated excerpts detached from date and recipient.
- Heroic anecdote without source passage context.
