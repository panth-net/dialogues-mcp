# Search Rules

Search by role, medium, conflict, and institution: `editor`, `newspaper`, `speech`, `lecture`, `abolition`, `slave`, `citizenship`, `Reconstruction`, `vote`, `rights`, `meeting`, `association`, `Republican`, `woman suffrage`.

| Path | Search vocabulary |
| --- | --- |
| douglass-as-strategist | `strategy OR party OR vote OR citizenship OR rights` |
| douglass-as-editor | `editor OR newspaper OR North Star OR paper OR publish` |
| movement-conflict | `Garrison OR disagreement OR convention OR party OR suffrage` |
| private-public-douglass | `private OR family OR letter OR confidential` |
| reconstruction-citizenship | `Reconstruction OR citizenship OR vote OR amendment OR freedmen` |
| lost-early-douglass | `fire OR Rochester OR destroyed OR missing OR early` |

Identify whether a result is correspondence, speech, article, newspaper item, draft, or family paper before interpreting it.
