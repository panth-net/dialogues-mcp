# Selection Rules

Select documents that reveal Douglass making arguments, building institutions, editing a public platform, answering conflict, or navigating Reconstruction.

High-signal:
- Douglass-authored letters, speeches, drafts, articles, and editorial work.
- Documents showing movement conflict, institutional strategy, or public/private differences.
- Reconstruction citizenship arguments and practical organizing.
- Family/private papers that reveal constraint, logistics, or public/private gaps.

Lower-signal:
- Generic mentions of Douglass.
- Later commemoration without direct relation to his working record.
- Metadata-only items unless useful as catalog anchors.
