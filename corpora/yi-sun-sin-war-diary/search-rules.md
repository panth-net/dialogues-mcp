# Search Rules

The downloaded text is the Classical Chinese (hanmun) of the 1795 jeonseo edition. Write terms naturally: `鳴梁` and the spaced form `"鳴 梁"` both work (the engine expands unspaced runs automatically). Keep terms short — two or three characters — and combine angles with OR. Korean and English searches only work against text you import yourself.

Good starting searches:

- `晴 OR 雨 OR 陰` (entries open with the weather)
- `"龜 船"` (turtle ships)
- `"閑 山" OR "見 乃 梁"` (Hansan)
- `"鳴 梁" OR "碧 波 亭"` (Myeongnyang)
- `"露 梁" OR "順 天"` (Noryang and the last campaign)
- `"元 均"` (Won Gyun)
- `母 OR 天 只` (his mother — the diary uses 天只)
- `"射 帿" OR 射` (archery practice)
- `"賊 船" OR 倭` (enemy ships)

Useful path strategies:

| Path | Search vocabulary |
| --- | --- |
| command-at-sea | `"龜 船" OR 陣 OR "格 軍" OR "賊 船" OR 戰` |
| court-and-injustice | `"元 均" OR 獄 OR "白 衣" OR 拿` |
| camp-life-day-by-day | `晴 OR 雨 OR 痛 OR 夢 OR 淚` |

Concept translations:

- "naval logistics" -> `"軍 糧" OR "格 軍" OR 船 OR 造` (provisions, oarsmen, shipbuilding)
- "military discipline" -> `斬 OR 杖 OR "軍 律"` (beheadings, floggings, military law)
- "illness and pain" -> `痛 OR 病 OR 不 平 OR 呻` 
- "family grief" -> `"天 只" OR 母 OR 葂 OR 夢 OR 慟` (mother, son Myeon, dreams, wailing)
- "court politics" -> `"朝 廷" OR 啓 OR "有 旨" OR 御` (court, dispatches, royal orders)
- "weather and omens" -> `風 OR 霧 OR 雷 OR 夢`

Dates are lunar and entries begin with the day's sexagenary cycle. If an event search fails, try the place name, then single distinctive characters, then the people involved.
