# Citation Rules

Cite the fetched passage ID and the entry date visible in the passage, and say whether the date is lunar (as in the original) or converted.

State which text the passage comes from: a Korean translation viewed via the heritage portal, the user-supplied Ha Tae-hung English translation, or another edition. The original is Classical Chinese, so every quotation in Korean or English is already a translation — say so when the wording carries the argument.

Never cite the heritage portal record or the LTI Korea catalog record as if they were diary text. Catalog records identify the material; they provide no passage evidence.

Where editions disagree or the autograph is damaged, note the uncertainty instead of picking a reading silently.
