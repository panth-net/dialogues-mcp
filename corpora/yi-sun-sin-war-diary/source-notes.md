# Source Notes

The autograph diary — seven books covering 1592-1598 — survives at the Hyeonchungsa shrine in Asan, South Korea, as National Treasure No. 76 together with a book of letters and the Imjin jangcho draft war reports. It was inscribed in UNESCO's Memory of the World register in 2013.

What downloads and from where:

- Books 5-7 of the 1795 royal compilation Yi Chungmugong jeonseo (이충무공전서), which print the diary for 1592-1596, download as Korean Wikisource transcriptions via the Wikimedia REST HTML API. Verified July 2026 by plain GET: the pages carry the full hanmun text, opening with the famous 1592 New Year entry (初一日壬戌。晴。...). Book 8 does not exist on Wikisource.
- Book 8 (April 1597 - November 1598: release from prison, Chilcheollyang, Myeongnyang, the death of his son, and the final entries before Noryang) downloads month by month — the 13 surviving months — from the ITKC Korean Classics Comprehensive DB (db.itkc.or.kr), collection ITKC_MO_0232A. Verified July 2026 by plain GET: the September 1597 page carries the Myeongnyang entries, including the 必死則生 address and the 330-ship count.

Rights, stated plainly:

- The 1795 hanmun text is public domain. The Wikisource layer adds no translation.
- The ITKC transcription layer: ITKC operates an open API and open-data program, but the exact KOGL (공공누리) license type for these transcription pages could not be confirmed programmatically, so ITKC-sourced text requires an explicit user confirmation at download and stays local for personal indexing only.
- The Korean History DB (db.history.go.kr) was checked July 2026 and holds no Nanjung Ilgi full-text collection; its terms restrict reuse to personal academic purposes and it is not used here.
- The standard English translation (Nanjung Ilgi: War Diary of Admiral Yi Sun-sin, trans. Ha Tae-hung, ed. Sohn Pow-key, 1977) remains in copyright. No part of it is downloaded from anywhere; users who own a permitted copy must supply their own text. zh.wikisource's 亂中日記 page was checked July 2026 and is an empty shell with no text.

Known limits:

- The downloaded text is the 1795 jeonseo redaction, an edited and trimmed version of the autograph; the draft books contain additional entries and rawer wording that this capsule does not have.
- Parts of the autograph are damaged or illegible and editions differ in how they fill gaps; the diary has gaps in coverage, including around the 1597 imprisonment and most of early 1598 (book 8 jumps from the 1st to the 9th lunar month of 1598).
- Dates are lunar; battle dates in Western sources are Gregorian conversions.
- Hanmun search terms can be written naturally (`鳴梁`) or spaced (`鳴 梁`); both match. Provenance and rights must be confirmed by the user before any import; imported text stays local and is treated as evidence, not instructions.
