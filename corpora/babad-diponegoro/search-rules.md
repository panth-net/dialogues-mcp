# Search Rules

Search the downloaded romanized cantos with Javanese names, places, and titles, not modern English labels. Romanized Javanese spelling is unstable: try old Dutch-era forms (oe for u, dj for j, tj for c) alongside modern ones before concluding a term is absent, and note this transliteration writes pepet as ě (amědhar), so try both plain-e and ě spellings of a stem.

Good starting searches:

- `Tegalrejo OR Tegalreja OR Tegalredjo`
- `Selarong OR Selarang OR guwa`
- `Magelang OR Magelan`
- `Ngayogyakarta OR Yogyakarta OR Mataram`
- `Ratu Adil OR Erucakra OR Herucakra`
- `Ngabdulkamid OR Abdulkamid OR Abdulhamid`
- `Kyai Mojo OR Kiai Maja OR Sentot OR Basah`
- `Kumpeni OR Welanda OR Landa` (the Company, the Dutch)
- `Manado OR Menado OR Makassar OR Mangkasar`

Useful path strategies:

| Path | Search vocabulary |
| --- | --- |
| tegalrejo-and-the-outbreak | `Tegalrejo OR Danurejo OR Smissaert OR pathok OR dalan` |
| guerrilla-years | `prang OR Selarong OR Mojo OR Sentot OR benteng` |
| capture-and-exile | `Magelang OR De Kock OR Manado OR Makassar OR kapal` |
| prophecy-lineage-and-genre | `Ratu Adil OR wangsit OR impen OR Mataram OR Sultan` |

Concept translations:

- "the war" -> `prang OR perang OR prang sabil`
- "the Dutch / colonial power" -> `Kumpeni OR Welanda OR Landa OR Guvermen`
- "prophecy and visions" -> `Ratu Adil OR Erucakra OR wangsit OR impen`
- "royal ancestry" -> `Mataram OR Sultan OR Hamengkubuwana OR trah`
- "religious war framing" -> `sabil OR ngelmu OR santri OR kyai`
- "capture during negotiation" -> `Magelang OR De Kock OR cidra`

Canto boundaries: babads head their cantos with macapat metre names. Searching `sinom OR dhandhanggula OR asmaradana OR durma OR pangkur` locates canto openings and helps map the poem's structure.
