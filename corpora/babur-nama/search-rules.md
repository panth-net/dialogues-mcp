# Search Rules

Use the vocabulary of Beveridge's translation — period names and her spellings — not modern labels.

Good starting searches:

- `Farghana OR Andijan OR Akhsi OR Samarkand OR Khujand`
- `Shaibaq OR Auzbeg OR siege OR fled OR throneless`
- `Kabul OR Ghazni OR garden OR orchard OR Herat`
- `Hindustan OR Panipat OR Ibrahim OR Agra OR Sanga`
- `melon OR grape OR mango OR pomegranate OR fruits`
- `wine OR party OR renounced OR repentance OR vow`
- `matchlock OR culverin OR carts OR array OR battle`
- `begs OR deserted OR oath OR pardoned OR loyalty`

Concept translations:

- "leadership in defeat" -> `fled OR throneless OR endured OR wept OR gathered`
- "military technology" -> `matchlock OR culverin OR carts OR guns OR array`
- "homesickness" -> `melon OR grape OR Kabul OR longing OR wept`
- "self-discipline" -> `wine OR renounced OR vow OR repentance OR broke`
- "natural history" -> `elephant OR peacock OR monkey OR flowers OR fruits`
- "court politics" -> `begs OR deserted OR oath OR pardoned OR treachery`

Beveridge's transliterations are idiosyncratic: Farghana (Fergana), Auzbeg (Uzbek), Shaibaq Khan (Shaibani Khan), Ibrahim Ludi (Lodi), Chin (China). Diacritics in the text (Babur as Bābur, Kabul as Kābul) still match plain-letter searches. If a modern name returns nothing, try Beveridge's spelling or a broader OR set.
