# Source Notes

The curated core is the complete Project Gutenberg edition of "The Babur-nama in English" (1912-1921), Annette Susannah Beveridge's translation from the Chagatai Turki original, made principally from the Haydarabad Codex.

Known limits of this edition:

- This is Beveridge's English translation, not the Chagatai original. The phrasing is hers ("begs", "Auzbeg", "throneless times"), she inserts frequent bracketed clarifications, and the Persian translation made for Akbar's court is a separate, different text not included here.
- There are gaps in the original manuscript record. The narrative breaks off between 1508 and 1519 and again between 1520 and 1525, and it stops abruptly in September 1529, more than a year before Babur's death in December 1530. Missing years are manuscript loss, not proof that nothing happened or that Babur stayed silent.
- Beveridge's footnotes, appendices, section headings, and translator's prefaces share the text stream with Babur's narrative; separate her apparatus from his words when quoting.
- Dates run on the Hijri calendar with Beveridge's inserted AD equivalents; a "year" in the text is a Hijri year that straddles two Western years.

Downloaded text is stored in the user's Dialogues data folder and indexed into a local search file. No source text or generated index is bundled.
