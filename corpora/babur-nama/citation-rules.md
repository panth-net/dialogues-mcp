# Citation Rules

Cite the fetched passage ID and the year or event visible in the passage, keeping the Hijri form when Beveridge shows it (e.g. "932 AH (1525-26 AD)").

Say that quotes come from Annette Beveridge's public-domain English translation of the Chagatai original when making broad claims; the English phrasing is hers, not Babur's own words, and fine points should not rest on a single translated word.

Separate Babur's narrative from Beveridge's footnotes, bracketed insertions, headings, and prefaces.

Before arguing from silence, note the manuscript gaps (1508-1519, 1520-1525, and everything after September 1529): an event missing from a search may sit in a lost stretch of the record.
