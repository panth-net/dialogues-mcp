# Search Rules

Search with campaign-era vocabulary, proper names, and Gandhi's own terms. The downloaded set covers 1884-1896, 1908, 1909-1911 (including Hind Swaraj), and 1930; Quit India (1942) has no downloaded text, so those searches return nothing unless the user imports vol. 76 manually.

Good starting searches:

- `satyagraha OR "passive resistance" OR resister OR pledge`
- `indentured OR coolie OR franchise OR Natal OR Transvaal`
- `registration OR certificate OR permit OR Asiatic OR Smuts`
- `salt OR Dandi OR march OR lawbreaking OR sedition`
- `Irwin OR Viceroy OR pact OR truce OR "civil disobedience"`
- `"Quit India" OR "do or die" OR August OR arrest OR detention`
- `khadi OR charkha OR spinning OR swadeshi OR boycott`
- `fast OR prayer OR ahimsa OR nonviolence OR truth`
- `Kasturba OR Harilal OR ashram OR Sabarmati OR Phoenix`

Useful path strategies:

| Path | Search vocabulary |
| --- | --- |
| south-africa-satyagraha | `Natal OR Transvaal OR indentured OR registration OR Smuts OR satyagraha` |
| salt-march-and-civil-disobedience | `salt OR Dandi OR march OR Irwin OR "civil disobedience" OR arrest` |
| quit-india | `"Quit India" OR "do or die" OR Linlithgow OR detention OR 1942` (pointer-only path: needs a manual vol. 76 import) |

Concept translations:

- "nonviolent protest" -> `satyagraha OR "passive resistance" OR "civil disobedience" OR ahimsa`
- "racial discrimination" -> `indentured OR coolie OR franchise OR Asiatic OR colour`
- "civil rights campaign" -> `petition OR registration OR permit OR deputation OR pledge`
- "economic self-reliance" -> `khadi OR charkha OR spinning OR swadeshi OR village`
- "hunger strike" -> `fast OR fasting OR penance OR vow`
- "independence movement" -> `swaraj OR "Quit India" OR Congress OR freedom`

Practical notes:

- Document headers in CWMG give type, recipient, and date ("LETTER TO...", "SPEECH AT...", "INTERVIEW TO..."); searching a recipient name in caps-style (`"LETTER TO KASTURBA"`) can land directly on documents.
- Gandhi's Gujarati and Hindi items appear in the editors' English translation; if a famous phrase returns nothing, try a looser paraphrase.
- OCR occasionally garbles ligatures and old type; try shorter stems when a distinctive word fails.
