# Citation Rules

Cite the fetched passage ID, the CWMG volume number, and the document header visible in the passage — its type, recipient or audience, and date (e.g. "Letter to Jawaharlal Nehru, 2 May 1930, CWMG vol. 43").

Name the document type in every answer: a private letter, a Young India or Harijan article, a public speech, a telegram, and a statement to government are different kinds of evidence.

When the passage is the editors' English translation of a Gujarati or Hindi original, say so; the translation choice introduces uncertainty that a broad claim should acknowledge.

Separate Gandhi's words from the editors' footnotes, source notes, and appendices printed in the same volumes.

Attribute the source as the Gandhi Heritage Portal's CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 hosting of the Collected Works of Mahatma Gandhi, and keep quoted extracts within personal, noncommercial use.
