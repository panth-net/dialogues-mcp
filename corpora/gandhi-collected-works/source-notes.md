# Source Notes

The source is the Collected Works of Mahatma Gandhi (CWMG), the English edition in about 100 volumes (Publications Division, Ministry of Information and Broadcasting, Government of India, 1956-1994). The authoritative hosting is the Gandhi Heritage Portal (gandhiheritageportal.org), a project of the Sabarmati Ashram Preservation and Memorial Trust; the downloadable text here comes from openly hosted archive.org scans of the same print edition, mostly Digital Library of India uploads.

What downloads and what does not:

- Five volumes (1, 8, 10, 43, 44) download as `_djvu.txt` OCR text from archive.org with a plain GET. Each was verified in July 2026 by fetching the file and checking the volume's own preface and period-specific content (franchise petitions in vol. 1, the 1908 Transvaal preface in vol. 8, the 1910 "Indian Home Rule" title page in vol. 10, the Salt March narrative in vol. 43, the Yeravda preface in vol. 44).
- Vols. 9 and 76 remain catalog pointers. The portal's per-volume "Download PDF" endpoint (`/downloadPDF/<id>`) is session-gated: a plain programmatic GET receives the HTML viewer instead of the PDF (verified July 2026). No open mirror of either volume verified: both archive.org uploads labeled "Vol IX" actually contain the February-June 1926 volume, the only "Vol 76" upload carries an unusable Devanagari-mode OCR layer over mismatched contents, the Digital Library of India run stops at vol. 74, and gandhiashramsevagram.in no longer resolves in DNS. Dialogues does not bypass session gating, so those two items give manual import instructions instead.

Rights, stated plainly:

- The portal publishes CWMG under Creative Commons BY-NC-ND 4.0. That permits downloading and local personal indexing with attribution, and it forbids commercial use, redistribution of the files, and republishing transformed versions of the edition. The download flow requires an explicit rights confirmation before fetching. Keep downloaded volumes local and cite the portal edition.
- CWMG's own copyright rests with the Government of India's Publications Division (by permission of the Navajivan Trust); the print edition scanned here is the original edition, not the withdrawn revised e-edition.

Known limits of this source:

- Many documents are the editors' English translation from Gujarati or Hindi originals; translation is flagged in the document headnotes, and answers should say when a quote is translated.
- The text is uncorrected OCR over old typesetting; expect character errors, hyphenation artifacts, and occasional garbled lines, and prefer distinctive proper names when a search fails.
- The edition gathers what survived: coverage is thinner for early years, and the volume prefaces, footnotes, and appendices (editors' work) share the text stream with Gandhi's own words.
- Volume-number labels on archive.org uploads are unreliable; each item URL here points at a specific verified file, not at a title search.
