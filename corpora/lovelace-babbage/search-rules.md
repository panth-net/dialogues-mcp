| --- | --- |
| lovelace-learning-math | `mathematics OR lesson OR De Morgan OR study OR calculation` |

| notes-before-computing-myth | `note OR operation OR symbol OR table OR Bernoulli` |
| program-traces-and-engine-design | `operation OR variable OR cards OR notation` |

For credit questions, start with transcript and folio vocabulary so primary
correspondence/transcription hits rank before secondary articles. Then widen to
`correspondence OR De Morgan OR Lovelace OR Babbage OR credit` if you need the
scholarly framing. Filter Lovelace vs Babbage vs De Morgan when metadata
supports author or correspondent fields.

Conceptual traps: do not search only for modern `programming`. Try source terms such as `operation`, `notation`, `cards`, `tables`, `calculation`, `engine`, and `variable`, then fetch exact passages before making a claim.
