# Selection Rules

Select documents that show mathematical learning, machine design, collaboration, notation, or contested credit.

High-signal:
- Lovelace-De Morgan mathematical learning and correspondence.
- Babbage notebooks, engineering drawings, and machine-design records.
- Menabrea translation and Notes contexts.
- Tables, operations, diagrams, and program-trace-like notations.
- Documents showing dependency, collaboration, revision, and credit conflict.

Lower-signal:
- Myth-making with no source-document support.
- Generic biography or later celebration.
- Metadata-only records unless they anchor drawings or missing transcriptions.
