# Source Notes

The curated core uses Clay Mathematics Institute Ada Lovelace materials and Science Museum Group Babbage collection records.

Clay items are PDFs: the folio-order transcripts, the early mathematical education article, and the Lovelace-De Morgan correspondence reappraisal. These are local user downloads only. The package ships URLs and curation metadata, not PDF text.

Science Museum items are public collection records for the Babbage Papers, Difference Engine records, Analytical Engine notations, card-counting apparatus, and related drawing/notation records. These usually provide catalog descriptions and image/document metadata, not full manuscript transcriptions.

Preserve author, correspondent, source collection, drawing/document URLs, archive identifiers, and whether evidence is transcription, article context, catalog description, object metadata, or user-supplied.
