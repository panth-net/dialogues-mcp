# Isaac Newton Papers

Use this corpus to study Newton through working documents: drafts, cancellations, corrections, alternate systems, and unfinished reasoning.

## Where to start

- Ask what Newton recorded day by day about "the matter in the glass" — a private laboratory diary in which he is "right sure" the thing inside is animated.
- Ask how the man who explained the planets spent years working out the end of the world from the prophecies.
- Ask what he answered when a young clergyman pressed him on whether gravity proves God — he grants "an intelligent agent," then sharply refuses a word being put in his mouth.
- Ask to see him alone in a darkened room with a prism — a draft in his own hand, corrections and all, of how he took light apart.

Do not treat amendments as noise. In this corpus, revision is often the point.

Avoid separating mathematics, optics, theology, chronology, and chymistry too cleanly before the documents justify it.
