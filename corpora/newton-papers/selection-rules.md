# Selection Rules

Select documents where reasoning develops across drafts, corrections, calculations, experiments, theological systems, chronology, or chymical procedures.

High-signal:
- Drafts, revisions, cancellations, alternate versions, and unfinished systems.
- Mathematical, experimental, theological, chronological, and chymical reasoning.
- Documents that show public/private dispute or publication decisions.

Lower-signal:
- Later summary without manuscript evidence.
- Metadata-only records unless they anchor an important missing manuscript.
