# Search Rules

Use period, work, family, and persona vocabulary.

Good starting searches:

- `printer OR pilot OR Nevada OR newspaper OR apprenticeship`
- `lecture OR travel OR Europe OR platform OR audience`
- `publisher OR proof OR book OR copyright OR printer`
- `money OR debt OR business OR machine OR investment`
- `Livy OR Susy OR Clara OR Jean OR family OR home`
- `Howells OR friendship OR editor OR criticism`
- `joke OR humor OR laugh OR persona OR Mark Twain`
- `grief OR death OR sorrow OR old OR bitterness`

Useful path strategies:

| Path | Search vocabulary |
| --- | --- |
| apprenticeship-and-voice | `printer OR pilot OR Nevada OR newspaper OR apprenticeship OR Clemens` |
| travel-lecture-and-public-persona | `lecture OR platform OR audience OR travel OR Europe OR Mark Twain` |
| authorship-and-publishing | `publisher OR proof OR book OR copyright OR printer OR Howells` |
| family-money-and-business-risk | `money OR debt OR business OR machine OR investment OR family` |
| grief-age-and-late-darkness | `grief OR death OR sorrow OR old OR bitterness OR late` |
| humor-private-public-gap | `Man OR joke OR Howells OR laugh OR bitter OR Clemens` |

Concept translations:

- "brand/persona" -> `Mark Twain OR Clemens OR platform OR lecture OR humor`
- "creative process" -> `book OR proof OR printer OR publisher OR chapter`
- "business failure" -> `debt OR machine OR investment OR money OR bankruptcy`
- "family life" -> `Livy OR Susy OR Clara OR Jean OR home`
- "late darkness" -> `grief OR death OR old OR sorrow OR bitterness`
- "funny or bitter" -> `joke OR humor OR laugh OR bitter OR persona OR Clemens`
- "business screwed him" -> `debt OR machine OR investment OR money OR bankruptcy`

Prefer path-specific searches when the user asks for a life phase or problem.

When a result begins with Paine's editorial narration, fetch neighboring hits
or nearby context until Twain/Clemens's own letter text is visible. Use the
editorial frame as context, not as the person's direct thought.
