# Selection Rules

Select records that show Twain/Clemens thinking and working across roles: apprentice, traveler, lecturer, author, husband, father, business risk-taker, public performer, friend, and older commentator.

High-signal:
- Letters with date/recipient context.
- Publisher, proof, copyright, lecture, or money evidence.
- Family, grief, friendship, and illness evidence.
- Passages where humor and practical pressure appear together.

Lower-signal:
- Audio-only records.
- Later biography without direct correspondence.
- Famous one-liners without surrounding letter context.
