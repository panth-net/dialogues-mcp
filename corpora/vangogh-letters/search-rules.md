# Search Rules

## Search By Person

- Theo: `Theo OR brother`
- Paul Gauguin: `Gauguin`
- Emile Bernard: `Bernard`
- Wil van Gogh: `Wil OR sister`

## Search By Theme

- Color and light: `color OR colour OR yellow OR blue OR light`
- Nature and landscape: `nature OR wheat OR cypress OR field OR sun`
- Work and materials: `work OR painting OR drawing OR canvas OR studio`
- Money and logistics: `money OR francs OR debt OR rent`
- Illness and care: `illness OR asylum OR doctor OR fatigue`
- Religion and consolation: `God OR religion OR faith OR consolation`
- Loneliness and friendship: `lonely OR loneliness OR friend OR friendship`

## Search By Genre

- Advice: `advice OR advise OR recommend`
- Confession: `confess OR confession OR admit`
- Art theory: `art OR theory OR colour OR composition`
- Daily logistics: `send OR receive OR enclosed OR parcel OR rent`
- Self-justification: `justify OR misunderstood OR explanation`

## Search By Chronology

Use metadata filters when the import provides dates. Otherwise search period/place language:

- Early letters: `London OR Hague OR Etten`
- Arles: `Arles OR yellow OR Gauguin`
- Saint-Remy: `Saint-Remy OR asylum OR cypress`
- Auvers: `Auvers OR doctor OR Gachet`

## Strategy

Search one concern at a time. Fetch passages before interpreting. Compare letters across recipients and dates when making broad claims. If metadata is absent or the import is partial, say so.
