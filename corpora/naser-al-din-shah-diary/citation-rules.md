# Citation Rules

Cite the fetched passage ID and locate the passage on the itinerary — the city or leg of the tour visible in or near the passage (e.g. "St. Petersburg", "the naval review in England", "Vienna, during the Exhibition").

Mention when making broad claims that the source is the 1874 Redhouse English translation of the Shah's Persian diary, published for European readers, and that quotations are translation, not the Shah's own words.

Flag OCR damage in a quotation rather than silently correcting it; if a passage is too garbled to quote responsibly, fetch neighboring context or say so.

Keep the genre visible: this is a monarch's account written for publication. Cite what the Shah says, and mark as interpretation any claim about what he privately thought or strategically omitted.
