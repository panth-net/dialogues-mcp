# Citation Rules

Cite the fetched passage ID, the work's title, and its date (e.g. "Notes on Nursing, 1860, chunk 12"). Quote from fetched passages only, never from search snippets.

Name the register: instructional handbook (Notes on Nursing), post-Crimea program (Subsidiary Notes), address to nurses, or statistical argument (Lying-in Institutions). Nightingale's tone shifts sharply between them.

Wellcome catalog records identify manuscript letters but contain no letter text; cite them as archive locations, never as quotations. When repeating her statistics, flag that plain-text tables may have lost layout and that her disease categories are period ones.
