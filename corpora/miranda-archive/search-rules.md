# Search Rules

Search the downloaded volumes with period proper names and Spanish, French, or English vocabulary as the document demands; the set is trilingual. Eighteenth-century spelling varies (Cádiz/Cadiz, Habana/Havana), and the OCR renders long-s as f in the 1794 and 1810s volumes (moft, fenfibly), so try variants before giving up.

Good starting searches:

- `Leander OR Coro OR Vela OR Ocumare`
- `Pensacola OR Habana OR Cagigal`
- `Catalina OR Rusia OR Kiev OR Potemkin`
- `Valmy OR Dumouriez OR Amberes OR Anvers`
- `San Mateo OR capitulación OR Monteverde`
- `La Guaira OR Carraca OR Cádiz OR prisión`
- `Hamilton OR Washington OR Knox OR Adams`
- `Caracas OR Venezuela OR independencia OR congreso`

Useful path strategies:

| Path | Search vocabulary |
| --- | --- |
| three-revolutions | `Pensacola OR Valmy OR Dumouriez OR ejército OR armée` |
| travel-diaries-and-networks | `diario OR viaje OR Catalina OR Hamilton OR Constantinopla` |
| leander-and-the-first-republic | `Leander OR Coro OR congreso OR terremoto OR capitulación` |
| archive-odyssey | `papeles OR archivo OR volumen OR Bathurst OR Sapphire` |

Concept translations:

- "revolutionary networking" -> `carta OR recomendación OR amistad OR sociedad`
- "military campaign" -> `ejército OR batalla OR sitio OR armée OR campagne`
- "independence plotting" -> `libertad OR independencia OR colonias OR América`
- "the betrayal at La Guaira" -> `La Guaira OR arresto OR capitulación OR Monteverde`
- "prison years" -> `prisión OR Carraca OR Cádiz OR castillo`
- "self-archiving" -> `papeles OR diario OR apuntes OR documentos`

If a Spanish search fails on a diary passage, retry in English or French: Miranda kept his American and Russian diaries partly in the language of the place, and letters arrive in the sender's tongue.
