# Citation Rules

Cite the fetched passage ID plus the letter date and addressee visible in the passage (e.g. "to Stella Zeehandelaar, Japara, 25 May 1899").

Always say which edition is being quoted: the Dutch "Door Duisternis tot Licht" (Gutenberg 35220) or the English "Letters of a Javanese Princess" (Symmers translation, Gutenberg 34647). When quoting the English, note that it is a translation; when a passage exists only in the Dutch collection, say so.

Both editions are Abendanon's selection — the English about two-thirds of the Dutch — so flag that filter before making broad claims about what Kartini thought, said, or never said.

Separate Kartini's words from the editorial apparatus: Abendanon's prefaces and notes, and Louis Couperus's foreword in the English edition.
