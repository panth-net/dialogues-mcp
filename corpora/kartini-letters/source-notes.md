# Source Notes

The curated core is two Project Gutenberg editions of the same correspondence:

- "Door Duisternis tot Licht: Gedachten over en voor het Javaansche volk" (1911, Dutch) — the letters as selected and edited by J.H. Abendanon, former Director of Education, Religion, and Industry in the Dutch East Indies.
- "Letters of a Javanese Princess" (1920, English) — Agnes Louise Symmers's translation of about two-thirds of the Dutch collection, with a foreword by Louis Couperus.

Known limits of these editions:

- The English edition omits about a third of the letters in the Dutch collection and often shortens the ones it keeps. Absence from an English search is not absence from the record; check the Dutch text before concluding a topic is missing.
- Both books pass through Abendanon's hands, and his selection has its own bias: he cut personal and family matters, softened passages that reflected on the colonial administration and on his own role in the abandoned scholarship plan, and shaped the book to serve a cause. The unabridged letters to the Abendanons were only published in 1987 (F.G.P. Jaquet's edition) and remain in copyright, so they are not included.
- Kartini wrote in Dutch, her second language, for Dutch readers; the English is a translation at a further remove, and its early-1900s renderings of Javanese terms are dated.
- Spelling is pre-reform Dutch (studeeren, Regeering) with period spellings of Javanese names (Japara for Jepara), and the editors' prefaces and Couperus's foreword share the text stream with the letters.
