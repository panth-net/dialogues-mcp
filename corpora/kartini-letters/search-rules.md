# Search Rules

Search in both languages: Dutch reaches the fuller original collection, English reaches the translation. Use period spellings (studeeren, Regeering, Japara), not modern Dutch or Indonesian.

Good starting searches:

- `kooi OR vrijheid OR vleugels OR verlangen`
- `cage OR freedom OR wings OR longing`
- `onderwijs OR school OR studeeren OR Holland`
- `education OR school OR study OR teacher`
- `vrouw OR vrouwen OR meisje OR huwelijk OR polygamie`
- `woman OR women OR girl OR marriage OR polygamy`
- `Javaan OR Javaansche OR volk OR adat OR Regent`
- `gamelan OR wajang OR batik OR houtsnijwerk OR carving`

Useful path strategies:

| Path | Search vocabulary |
| --- | --- |
| seclusion-and-freedom | `kooi OR vrijheid OR vleugels OR cage OR freedom OR longing` |
| education-and-the-school-plan | `onderwijs OR studeeren OR Holland OR education OR school OR teacher` |
| colonial-java | `Regeering OR bestuur OR opium OR volk OR government OR people` |
| marriage-and-polygamy | `huwelijk OR polygamie OR bruid OR marriage OR polygamy OR wife` |
| javanese-art-and-culture | `gamelan OR wajang OR batik OR houtsnijwerk OR carving OR artist` |

Concept translations:

- "women's rights" -> `vrouwen OR emancipatie OR women OR emancipation`
- "colonial critique" -> `Regeering OR opium OR volk OR government OR Hollanders`
- "arranged marriage" -> `huwelijk OR trouwen OR bruid OR marriage OR wedding`
- "religion and belief" -> `God OR godsdienst OR Islam OR geloof OR religion`
- "family duty" -> `vader OR moeder OR adat OR ouders OR father OR mother OR custom`
- "craft and heritage" -> `batik OR houtsnijwerk OR gamelan OR carving OR wood`

Names to search directly: Stella, Moedertje, Roekmini, Kardinah, Japara, Rembang, Semarang, Batavia, Abendanon, van Kol. The same letter may exist in Dutch and English with different wording — fetch both when comparing, and remember that a term missing in English may still exist in the omitted Dutch letters.
