# Source Notes

Faraday's downloadable starter core uses public-domain Project Gutenberg texts:

- `Experimental Researches in Electricity, Volume 1`
- `The Chemical History of a Candle`
- `On the Various Forces of Nature and Their Relations to Each Other`

These are useful for experiment, apparatus, public lecture, induction, force, and demonstration vocabulary. They are not laboratory notebooks, correspondence transcripts, or surviving lecture-note manuscripts.

The Royal Institution and AIM25 records map the manuscript and object surface: laboratory notebooks, lecture notes, correspondence, apparatus, the preserved magnetic laboratory, and access conditions. Those records are `catalog_only` until permitted transcripts or local user-supplied files are available.

Do not package restricted modern editions of Faraday's diary, correspondence, or microfilm material. If a transcript source is not cleanly permitted, keep the item catalog-only or user-supplied.

Distinguish notebooks, correspondence, lecture notes, demonstrations, apparatus records, and later edited publications in every answer.
