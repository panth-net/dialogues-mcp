# Selection Rules

Select documents that show observation, repetition, apparatus, demonstration, correspondence, and revision.

High-signal:
- Notebooks and experimental records.
- Instrument descriptions, diagrams, apparatus notes, and repeated trials.
- Lecture notes as self-education or public demonstration.
- Letters coordinating demonstrations or responding to objections.

Lower-signal:
- Later edited publications without working evidence.
- Catalog-only records unless they identify essential notebook or correspondence targets.
