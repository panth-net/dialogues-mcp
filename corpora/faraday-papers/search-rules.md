# Search Rules

Search by experimental action and apparatus: `experiment`, `notebook`, `instrument`, `lecture`, `demonstration`, `electricity`, `magnet`, `trial`, `observed`, `repeat`, `apparatus`, `induction`, `galvanometer`, `electro-magnetic`, `lines of force`, `candle`, `force`.

| Path | Search vocabulary |
| --- | --- |
| experimental-thinking | `experiment OR trial OR observed OR repeat OR result OR induction` |
| notebooks-and-instruments | `notebook OR apparatus OR instrument OR diagram OR galvanometer OR magnet` |
| lecture-notes-as-self-education | `lecture OR notes OR audience OR demonstration OR candle OR force` |
| letters-and-demonstration | `letter OR correspondence OR demonstration OR request OR reply OR lighthouse` |
| electricity-before-theory | `electricity OR magnetism OR current OR induction OR electro-magnetic OR "lines of force"` |
| public-science | `lecture OR "Royal Institution" OR audience OR public OR candle OR demonstration` |

For manuscript and object questions, search the guide and source notes first, then search/fetch the indexed public-domain texts. If the needed evidence is only represented by a catalog-only record, answer with that limitation rather than inventing passage evidence.
