# Search Rules

Two layers are searchable here. The works-in-translation path downloads English translations of the Guide for the Perplexed and the Eight Chapters, so those searches work as soon as the download has run. Geniza fragment searches serve users who have imported permitted transcriptions or translations; that layer is empty until then, and should be searched in the language of the imported files.

Good starting searches in the downloaded works:

- `perplexed OR guide OR treatise`
- `Aristotle OR philosophers OR Metaphysics`
- `prophecy OR prophet OR Moses`
- `providence OR evil OR creation OR eternity`
- `attributes OR incorporeal OR anthropomorphism`
- `soul OR virtue OR mean OR disease` (Eight Chapters)
- `"free will" OR choice OR fatalism` (Eight Chapters)

Good starting searches for imported Geniza material:

- `David OR brother OR drowned OR ship OR India`
- `Aydhab OR Red Sea OR voyage OR merchant`
- `responsum OR query OR question OR answer OR ruling`
- `Mishneh Torah OR halakhah OR draft OR correction`
- `Guide OR perplexed OR philosophy OR intellect`
- `physician OR medicine OR regimen OR sultan OR court`
- `Fustat OR synagogue OR community OR captives OR ransom`
- `dinar OR payment OR debt OR partnership`

Useful path strategies:

| Path | Search vocabulary |
| --- | --- |
| works-in-translation | `Aristotle OR prophecy OR attributes OR providence OR virtue OR mean` |
| drafts-and-works | `draft OR correction OR Mishneh OR Guide OR commentary OR chapter` |
| letters-and-loss | `David OR brother OR ship OR grief OR letter OR Aydhab` |
| physician-and-community | `physician OR responsum OR community OR captives OR ransom OR court` |

Concept translations:

- "how his books were written" -> `draft OR correction OR margin OR autograph OR copy`
- "family tragedy" -> `David OR brother OR drowned OR mourning OR India`
- "medical practice" -> `physician OR regimen OR remedy OR patient OR sultan`
- "community leadership" -> `head OR judge OR elders OR appeal OR ruling OR charity`
- "trade and money" -> `dinar OR merchant OR partnership OR goods OR ship`
- "religious authority" -> `responsum OR query OR halakhah OR permitted OR forbidden`

Fragment language is formulaic: letters open with blessings and honorifics. If a name search fails, try the genre word (`query`, `letter`, `list`) or the place name.

In the downloaded translations, search Friedländer's and Gorfinkle's English vocabulary, not modern scholarly terms: `incorporeal` rather than "apophatic", `Law of Moses` rather than "halakhah" for the Guide's usage. The Eight Chapters file is an OCR scan; if a term fails there, try a shorter stem or a synonym before concluding it is absent.
