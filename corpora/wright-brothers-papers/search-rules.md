# Search Rules

Search with the brothers' period vocabulary: they say "flying machine" and "aeroplane" (not airplane), "screw" or "propeller", "motor" (not engine, usually), "warping" for roll control, and "drift" for drag.

Good starting searches:

- `glider OR gliding OR soaring OR coast`
- `Kitty Hawk OR "Kill Devil" OR camp OR sand`
- `lift OR drift OR pressure OR tables OR angle`
- `rudder OR warping OR tail OR control OR balance`
- `motor OR propeller OR screw OR power OR thrust`
- `Lilienthal OR Chanute OR Langley OR Maxim OR Pilcher`
- `wind OR velocity OR miles an hour OR anemometer`
- `December OR 1903 OR first flight OR started`

Concept translations:

- "aerodynamics" -> `lift OR drift OR pressure OR resistance OR angle of incidence`
- "flight control" -> `warping OR rudder OR balance OR equilibrium OR steering`
- "wind tunnel research" -> `tables OR measurements OR surfaces OR pressures OR apparatus`
- "test flights" -> `trials OR glides OR starts OR landed OR broke`
- "aviation pioneers" -> `Lilienthal OR Pilcher OR Chanute OR Langley OR Ader OR Maxim`
- "crash" -> `broke OR wrecked OR smash OR accident OR repairs`
- "engine design" -> `motor OR horse-power OR cylinders OR gasoline OR weight`

The Chanute survey is OCR text: expect occasional scrambled characters, and prefer distinctive proper names (Lilienthal, Hargrave, Penaud) over short common words when searching it.
