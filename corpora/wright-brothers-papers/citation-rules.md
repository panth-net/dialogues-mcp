# Citation Rules

Cite the fetched passage ID, the text's title, its author (Wilbur, Orville, both, or Chanute), and its original publication date.

Distinguish the three layers in every answer: (1) the brothers' published retrospective accounts (1901 lecture, 1908 article, 1913 narrative), (2) Chanute's 1894 pre-Wright survey, and (3) Library of Congress catalog records for the manuscript diaries and letters, which identify documents but contain no passage text and must never be quoted as if they did.

Quote from fetched passages only. When citing the Chanute text, note that it is OCR of a scanned book and verify garbled numbers before repeating them.
